The series comes from watching temporary bamboo scaffolding at permaculture sites. For labourers it meant work and livelihood; for children the same structures became places to play. That coexistence of labour and leisure in one form is where the series began.

Rope is not just a connector but a way of stabilising and negotiating balance. Bamboo meets at irregular angles, and it is the repeated binding that lets the fragile joints hold.
