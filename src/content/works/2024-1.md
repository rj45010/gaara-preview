The Vangrah series explores how fragile structures can sustain themselves while remaining open, porous and adaptive. Informed by root networks and living root bridges, it understands strength as something that develops through interconnection rather than density.

Central to the work are hidden cavities. During making, these spaces stay filled and concealed, so the form appears solid. Only after firing are the voids revealed, suggesting spaces of shelter and habitation. The work shifts from being constructed to being uncovered.
