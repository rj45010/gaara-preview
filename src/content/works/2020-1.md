Vinyasa sits about 20 km from Jaipur in a village named Rojda, an ashram built from natural materials. I designed seven cottages there, each with its own theme: Shoonya, Jal, Chandrama, Vaayu, Prithvi, Agni and Maati.

Shown at Rojda, near Jaipur.
