A study of whether fragility, when repeated with precision, can begin to function as structure. Each line is fragile on its own, yet through accumulation the surface begins to hold, creating an impression of strength.

The process requires careful calibration of slip consistency, moisture, timing and spacing. Time and climate become active conditions, influencing drying, cracking and adhesion. This series became the foundation for my architectural fragments, a shift from surface to structure.
