Could I preserve the spontaneity of the bamboo structure in a rigid material? Instead of conventional casting, I took a direct mould of a bamboo-and-rope sculpture and let the materials burn out in the kiln, leaving a cavity. I poured metal directly into the space, letting it spill and take its own shape.

The sculpture became a fossilised trace of that playfulness, a permanent memory of an impermanent act.
