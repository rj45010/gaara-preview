As the series evolves, the sculptural studies expand into life-size installations. The structures are not pre-designed but grow intuitively through tying, layering and responding to material behaviour. Woven rope sections suggest areas of rest, inviting the body into the work.

Anandam proposes architecture as an experiential and adaptive form, one that is not fixed but responsive, shaped through use, perception and interaction.

Shown at MS University, Baroda.
