This installation uses the herringbone pattern, commonly found in design and tiling as a repetitive system of order and unit formation. Through block printing, the pattern is built over time, registering duration, fatigue, pressure and variation.

I disrupt this system by introducing voids and breaks, challenging its assumed stability. Within these fractures, sculptural fragments emerge, suggesting forms that gather, shift and accumulate into mass. They remain unresolved, pointing toward a biomimetic architecture that evolves through breakdown, adaptation and becoming.

Shown at Thesis show, Shiv Nadar University.
